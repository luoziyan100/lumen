# objects/ — White-Porcelain Architectural CAD Scholarly Instruments
> L2 | Parent: ../CLAUDE.md

Each object fills one empty state with a bespoke **Monochrome White-Porcelain × Hairline CAD** precision instrument (95%+ sculpted warm-white porcelain & architectural stone-gray `0.55–0.85px` hairlines, zero cartoon color-blocking, ~3% restrained functional accent). Each of the 8 objects has its own distinct mechanical archetype and silhouette; none carries a control.

- `instrument.ts`: shared CAD primitives (`r2`, `bolt`, `screw`, `rivet`, `rivetRow`, `gearPath`, `trussArm`, `prism` (the one oblique projection for volume: front, top and right faces), `coolingFins`, `vernierRing`, `instrumentDefs`, `dotGridBackdrop`, `spinKeyframes`) with per-object `<defs>` id prefixes so simultaneously mounted instruments never collide.
- `iso.ts`: text & escaping utilities (`text`, `escapeXml`, `textWidth`, `wrapText`, `css`). `text()` escapes `& < > " '`, so dynamic titles/takeaways/labels enter SVG as text only.
- `solid.ts`: legacy 3D projection helper retained for backwards reference.
- `box.ts` (`bx-`, `260×220`): **VAULT · I** takeaway box under Topics. The lid turns on one hinge line: hover cracks it, open folds its top face onto the hinge and then raises its inner face; the side winding wheel turns with it. The front card carries `boxWords` (stamp + 2 lines).
- `cabinet.ts` (`cb-`, `280×340`): **ARCH · VI** index cabinet in empty Materials. Plinth, body and cornice are `prism` blocks with one depth; the microfilm reels stand on the cornice's top face. Five drawers are flush with shadowed reveals; the middle-left one is a real box (slot, tray, side, standing cards) that slides out toward the viewer along −depth, clipped to where it is outside the cabinet.
- `typewriter.ts` (`tw-`, `640×400`): **LUMEN · IV** optical reading typewriter in empty Reading (`OPT.01` dial, sheet with `paperLines(title)`, magnifier arm resting below the title, amber/celadon flasks, `.is-typing` loop). Ink follows the keys: cipher lines write only while typing and stand finished otherwise.
- `easel.ts` (`ea-`, `640×400`): **DRAFT · II** overhead gantry plotter in empty Board. The pen draws one closed hypotrochoid; gantry X, carriage Y and both arm angles are solved from the curve per keyframe, so the nib stays on the growing line. The dashed circles are that curve's construction; the stepper's belt is the two true tangents of its pulleys.
- `press.ts` (`pr-`, `640×400`): **SEAL · III** nipping press with motor and turbine in empty Takeaways. Screw, T-handle and platen travel as one piece through the crown's nut; at rest the plate presses the folio, open lifts it clear to show the pressed leaf and seal on the folio's top face; the handle turns on every change.
- `corkboard.ts` (`ck-`, `640×400`): **ORBIT · V** orrery for the Map placeholder. Six nodes on one orbit; the hub gear carries an amber sweep and each node flashes as the sweep's edge reaches it (one 12s clock). Open pulls the slack chord taut.
- `lamp.ts` (`lm-`, `640×400`): **SPARK · VI** optical bench for the Ideas placeholder. Off at rest; hover throws the base switch and the bulb flickers on before its beam reaches the collector. The shade hangs on the arm's head pivot and aims at the collector; beam, rim and pool are solved from that pivot. A cable runs clipped along both arms into the head. Gradient-only light (no blur), rising motes, knurled micrometer slide.
- `typecase.ts` (`tc-`, `640×400`): **INDEX · VII** belt conveyor, sorter arm and index console for the All placeholder. One 6s cause chain: the belt advances one carton and stops, the scan head dips onto it, a pulse runs down the cable, the register's last wheel steps and the bay sweeps. Open lifts 5 archive cards.
- `IsoObject.tsx`: memoized React host (`React.memo` + `useMemo` for `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`) so polling re-renders never reset SVG animations.
