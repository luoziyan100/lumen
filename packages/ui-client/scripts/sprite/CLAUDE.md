- `sprite-fixture.ts`：显式临时根上的真实服务，仅替换 provider 网络；逐段推进文字与提问，按资源统计真正 GET SSE。
- `sprite-move-assert.mjs`：两档视口的全部拖放、开合、层级与减弱动效回归，横纵按60×92分别计算。Since the canvas (2026-11-19) the first sheet is ready when its canvas takes input.
- `assert-env.ts`: the guard shared by the suites that wipe a fixture root and start their own vite (sediment, reading, objects, lifecycle). `LUMEN_ASSERT_ROOT` overrides each suite's data root (default unchanged, only `/tmp/lumen-*`); give every worktree its own root. Before wiping anything, `lsof` must find nothing listening on PORT, else the run stops with the holder's pid: a stale `--hold` vite answers the readiness probe and sends the pages to the wrong fixture.
- `ui-assert-sediment.mts`: data root and port guarded by `assert-env.ts`. Since the canvas (2026-11-19) the model draws skeletons, the row comes only with an invite, and each pass seeds an old block with its takeaway: the chip is title only and does not jump, and deleting that sheet still counts the takeaway. P1-1 浏览器验收：自带隔离 fixture（画满/邀请/递回）+ 5190 vite，两档视口（暗色在 1280）+ 英文轮 + 截图进 evidence；结束前重跑 `ui-assert-panels.mjs`（其 R16/R17 断言不变，材料种子为标题 1706.03762 的合成 PDF）。
- `ui-assert-reading.mts`: P1-2 & living-reading explorable browser acceptance with its own isolated fixture (a nine-section reading with an interactive 2D SVG/range widget in section 2 seeded through the store; the model writes a notes reading, answers questions and replies to widget messages) and vite (PORT, default 5190): Chromium 8 passes (1440×900 and 1280×800 × light/dark × zh/en) plus `--webkit` 2 passes (1440×900 light zh, 1280×800 dark en). Reading first and default, the empty state as the typewriter alone, the header with the title only, prose centred within 700px inside a 960px column while widgets widen with zero overflowX, numbered sections and chips, in-view scrolling with panes and headers unmoved, no text line in view under the sprite's figure (top and bottom of the reading), real mouse selections opening the window below (or above near the bottom) without covering the sentence, elementFromPoint on the window, input and send, focus in the input, the reply in the window, a footprint on a sentence that starts mid-line and wraps leaves the paragraph's line count unchanged with its first line box on the line of the text before it (G4; elementFromPoint tests every line box of a wrapped inline element), footprints rebuilt across page loads, in-place widget expand/collapse (position:fixed solid light stage above side panes/sprite, state preserved, single iframe load, 8b13fde user-activation gate + R13 marker in expanded state, inline iframe Esc no-op, button + iframe-focused Esc restore height & scrollTop, split-reader convergence), and the live write from the Materials row once. Screenshots go to the evidence directory.
- `ui-assert-objects.mts`: browser acceptance for the objects in the product, with its own isolated fixture (the model holds the first section until the script opens a gate) and vite (PORT, default 5191). It covers 1440×900 and 1280×800 × light/dark × zh/en. Each object shows only in its empty state: the easel alone and centred on the canvas with no sheet and on a live sheet with no stroke (V0; over a canvas strokes go through it, so only the no-sheet easel is hovered and opened); the cabinet alone and centred in the materials pane; the typewriter, the press (V1) and the cork board, the lamp and the type case (V2–V4) each alone in their empty view: 280–320 wide, centre within 8px of the view's, no visible text outside the drawing, no button. The press goes and 「写一条沉淀」 returns with one takeaway; the placeholders are named 「还没开放」 for screen readers only. The controls beside the objects (the Materials row's 写解读, upload, 写一条沉淀 with a takeaway, every view tab, ＋, the lone tab and its 「⋯」, Excalidraw's zoom, Settings, every topic) pass elementFromPoint, and the lamp's pool overlaps no text or control. Objects are aria-hidden, untabbable and never take focus. The box's card carries the newest takeaway, and a click on the box changes nothing else. Two captures 1 s apart are identical at rest, hovered and opened (Chromium `--disable-gpu`). Reduced motion leaves no transition or animation over 100ms. Typing runs between the row's request and the first section, with no state line. One sheet shows one tab whose 「⋯」 still deletes it (V8). The box's boundary is measured, not assumed: topics are added one at a time, and at 1440×900 and 1440×700 the script records the last count that still shows the box and the first that folds it. At 700's boundary count, dragging 900→700→900 in 20px steps folds and restores the box once each way. At every step the box shows exactly when the free height fits. The first and last topic rows and Settings move 0px. V6 runs in Chromium and WebKit, zh and en (`nav-tips.ts`). Console errors fail the run, except the dev server's favicon. WebKit captures every object at rest and open. `--no-easel` skips the easel. Do not edit source while it runs: vite's reload replaces the page mid-capture.
- `ui-assert-lifecycle.mts`: U1–U8 browser acceptance with its own isolated fixture (the model holds a run on 等一下 and asks on 问我) and vite (PORT, default 5193), root guarded by `assert-env.ts` (default /tmp/lumen-lc-data). Four passes (1440×900 and 1280×800 × light/dark, zh and en), each on its own seeded topics: the 「⋯」 menu by mouse and keyboard on both lists, rename by menu and double click (Enter, Esc, blur, empty), Copy ID exact on the clipboard, busy rows with 归档/删除 disabled and the reason, archive and delete moving the selection, the delete dialog (one line, 取消 focused, Esc), Settings › 已归档 unarchive and delete, no 「新对话」 button in the list. A late 404 is allowed only for the URLs of what was just deleted. WebKit captures the topic menu, the dialog and Settings › 已归档. `ONLY=<tag>` runs one pass for debugging.
- `ui-assert-board.mts`: the co-drawn canvas's browser acceptance (task 2026-11-19-lumen-board-excalidraw). Serves a production build (OUTDIR, default /tmp/lumen-bx-build-branch) with `vite preview` (PORT, default 5196) against its own fixture with vision on (default /tmp/lumen-board-data, guarded by `assert-env.ts`); sends go through the sprite's composer, since the board refetches on the conversation's tool signal. Chromium 1440×900 zh light and 1280×800 en dark (both panes open), WebKit 1440×900: library/Help/sticky note hidden, no diagnostics or seed, every editor and strip control hit-tests as itself, islands inside the canvas and clear of the strip and pane headers, solid islands/menu/strip/banner; a user rectangle saved and back after a reload; the viewport saved after a scroll; a board_draw inside the viewport, laid out with the page's Excalifont and Xiaolai from `/excalidraw/fonts/`, committed as the AI's, its arrow bound to the user's rectangle; the glance current after the AI's write and before a sprite send; board_look updated and the request after it carrying one image (never more than one per request); board_erase's banner, restore and keep. Any console error once the board opens fails the run: the build strips Excalidraw's esm.sh font fallback (vite.config.ts, excalidraw/excalidraw#12609); page-load messages are listed as INFO. The phase-0 probes (`probe-excalidraw*.mts`) retired with the probe flag; their evidence stays in the task directory.
- `nav-tips.ts`: V6's measurement, shared by both browsers. It narrows the window until the navigation shows icons only, parks the sprite right under it (seeded anchor, then reload), and times each tab's Kumo tooltip from just before the input to the first frame that holds the label, by hover and by keyboard Tab. Each tooltip must sit under its tab, inside the window, and be the topmost element at its centre; at least one must overlap the sprite, so "above the sprite" is tested, not assumed.

- `ui-assert-panels.mjs`：上一任务全部面板回归，形象尺寸改为60×92；视图导航加图标后「每项一行」只量文字行数。 Since R16 sheet tabs are a glyph plus a number, W1 "one line per tab" measures only the number span. - `sprite-native.swift`：CGWindowList 量窗口，AX/合成事件驱动原生用户路径与 Dock 退出菜单；不需要屏幕录制，拖动需要辅助功能权限。
- `sprite-native-assert.mjs`：默认验证H1–H4：只创建主WebView、开关隐藏、关窗/最小化不浮出；恢复两端常量后可加--desktop-enabled跑原完整桌面路径。
- `trace/`：仅调试构建编译的原生取证模块，观察主窗原生投影与桌面真实 DOM 状态及跨窗口时延，不改变产品协议。
- `naming.mjs`：浏览器和原生共用的命名审计，检查变更源码、生产网页与原生可打印字符串。

- Language (i18n): `ui-assert-panels.mjs` and `sprite-move-assert.mjs` take `LOCALE=en|zh` (default zh) and `PORT` (default 5183). The language is seeded before the page loads, and every visible word they click or compare comes from that locale's catalog, so both pass in either language with unchanged conditions. Check names stay Chinese.

运行于仓库根：先编译 Swift 驱动到 `/tmp/lumen-sprite-native`，启动 fixture 和5183，再依次执行原生与浏览器断言，避免受控模型流互相覆盖。输出目录从参数传入，证据不进入产品。
