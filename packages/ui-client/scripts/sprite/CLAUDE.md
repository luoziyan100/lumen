# sprite/ — 一体两形验收
> L2 | 父级: ../CLAUDE.md

- `sprite-fixture.ts`：显式临时根上的真实服务，仅替换 provider 网络；逐段推进文字与提问，按资源统计真正 GET SSE。
- `sprite-move-assert.mjs`：两档视口的全部拖放、开合、层级与减弱动效回归，横纵按60×92分别计算。
- `ui-assert-sediment.mts`: `LUMEN_ASSERT_ROOT` overrides its data root, which it wipes at start; give every worktree its own root. P1-1 浏览器验收：自带隔离 fixture（画满/邀请/递回）+ 5190 vite，两档视口（暗色在 1280）+ 英文轮 + 截图进 evidence；结束前重跑 `ui-assert-panels.mjs`（其 R16/R17 断言不变，材料种子为标题 1706.03762 的合成 PDF）。
- `ui-assert-reading.mts`: P1-2 browser acceptance with its own isolated fixture (a nine-section reading seeded through the store; the model writes a notes reading and answers questions) and vite (PORT, default 5190): 1440×900 and 1280×800 × light/dark × zh/en. Reading first and default, the empty line, the ≤700px serif column, numbered sections and chips, in-view scrolling with panes and headers unmoved, no text line in view under the sprite's figure (top and bottom of the reading), real mouse selections opening the window below (or above near the bottom) without covering the sentence, elementFromPoint on the window, input and send, focus in the input, the reply in the window, a footprint on a sentence that starts mid-line and wraps leaves the paragraph's line count unchanged with its first line box on the line of the text before it (G4; elementFromPoint tests every line box of a wrapped inline element), footprints rebuilt across page loads, and the live write from the Materials row once. Screenshots go to the evidence directory.
- `ui-assert-objects.mts`: browser acceptance for the objects in the product, with its own isolated fixture (the model holds the first section until the script opens a gate) and vite (PORT, default 5191). It covers 1440×900 and 1280×800 × light/dark × zh/en. Each object shows only in its empty state. The controls beside the objects (写解读, upload, ＋, Settings, every topic) pass elementFromPoint. Objects are aria-hidden, untabbable and never take focus. The box's card carries the newest takeaway, and a click on the box changes nothing else. Two captures 1 s apart are identical at rest, hovered and opened (Chromium `--disable-gpu`). Reduced motion leaves no transition or animation over 100ms. Typing runs between the request and the first section. The box's boundary is measured, not assumed: topics are added one at a time, and at 1440×900 and 1440×700 the script records the last count that still shows the box and the first that folds it. At 700's boundary count, dragging 900→700→900 in 20px steps folds and restores the box once each way. At every step the box shows exactly when the free height fits. The first and last topic rows and Settings move 0px. The easel stands under the no-sheet line, which keeps its place, at most 320 wide and clear of the zoom bar. Console errors fail the run, except the dev server's favicon. WebKit captures every object at rest and open. `--no-easel` skips the easel. Do not edit source while it runs: vite's reload replaces the page mid-capture.
- Since P1-2, Reading is the default view: `ui-assert-panels.mjs`, `sprite-move-assert.mjs` and `ui-assert-sediment.mts` click the Board view after picking the topic; their assertions are unchanged.
- `ui-assert-panels.mjs`：上一任务全部面板回归，形象尺寸改为60×92；视图导航加图标后「每项一行」只量文字行数。 Since R16 sheet tabs are a glyph plus a number, W1 "one line per tab" measures only the number span. Since R17 each tab also holds a 「⋯」 menu button, so tab buttons are selected as `.board-tab > button:first-child` (here and in `sprite-move-assert.mjs`). G4 (four font sizes) skips `.iso-host`: the objects' drawn labels are illustration, like Mermaid and KaTeX, not UI type.
- `sprite-native.swift`：CGWindowList 量窗口，AX/合成事件驱动原生用户路径与 Dock 退出菜单；不需要屏幕录制，拖动需要辅助功能权限。
- `sprite-native-assert.mjs`：默认验证H1–H4：只创建主WebView、开关隐藏、关窗/最小化不浮出；恢复两端常量后可加--desktop-enabled跑原完整桌面路径。
- `trace/`：仅调试构建编译的原生取证模块，观察主窗原生投影与桌面真实 DOM 状态及跨窗口时延，不改变产品协议。
- `naming.mjs`：浏览器和原生共用的命名审计，检查变更源码、生产网页与原生可打印字符串。

- Language (i18n): `ui-assert-panels.mjs` and `sprite-move-assert.mjs` take `LOCALE=en|zh` (default zh) and `PORT` (default 5183). The language is seeded before the page loads, and every visible word they click or compare comes from that locale's catalog, so both pass in either language with unchanged conditions. Check names stay Chinese.

运行于仓库根：先编译 Swift 驱动到 `/tmp/lumen-sprite-native`，启动 fixture 和5183，再依次执行原生与浏览器断言，避免受控模型流互相覆盖。输出目录从参数传入，证据不进入产品。
